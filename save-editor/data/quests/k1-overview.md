## What is uncertain everywhere

- Every file has `checked`: false. Only the Endar Spire skip has been played.
- Story values come from reading the scripts; where the maintainer's saves cover a chapter (GalaxyMap after Dantooine, Game224 on Tatooine, Game22 on Manaan, Game133 late on Taris) the values were compared with them, and the Endar Spire milestone matched the GalaxyMap save except END_BRIDGE_SEALED (the save predates K1 Community Patch) and END_TRASK_DLG (32: the footlocker was opened once).
- Side quests are not completed by a milestone: their globals stay unchanged (value null) and the quest database can finish them one by one.
- The XP scenarios are estimates (tools/xp/make_path.py): they count kills, journal XP and the plot XP of journal steps on one route per quest, and miss plot XP on dialog lines that set no journal stage, level-scaled computer XP and stealth XP. The editor only raises XP to xp_at_end.
- The cleared sections: certain = false entries (removed by a script off the critical path, unlocked doors assumed walked through, locals set to more than one value) need a choice by the editor; locals on creatures are not listed.
- The four free planets and the Leviathan: in the game the Leviathan comes after the third star map, so the chapter order 4 to 8 is the list's order, not a fixed story order.
